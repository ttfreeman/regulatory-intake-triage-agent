# AER Build Challenge: candidate pack

Contents:

- `AER Build Challenge - Candidate Brief.docx` - what to build, what to bring, and how your session will run
- `intake_records.json` - 40 synthetic intake records
- `directives_extract.md` - six synthetic regulatory extracts
- `routing_rules.md` - the current human triage process

Everything in this pack is fabricated. No AER data, no real operators, no real people,
no real directives. Please do not use any data from your current or former employer in
this exercise, and do not attempt to access any AER system.

There is nothing to submit in advance. Bring your working solution to your interview on
your own laptop, ready to run; you will be handed a small further set of records in the
session and asked to run them live. Your interview time, panel, and the cutoff for
questions are in the email that came with this pack.
